VarkaEvaluatorOverheadBenchmark: the evaluators before and after VARKA-251's split, ten runs
of each at each vector width, on one machine (an AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX PRO 370, JDK 25, pinned as
dev/varka_bench_regen.sh pins it). "master" is commit 7e8faaa2b21, the evaluators as they were;
"split" is commit 644a2faa36b, VarkaEvaluatorBase composed of the Java components. Each side's
ten runs came from dev/varka_bench_repeat.sh; lo is the case's minimum over the ten, hi its
maximum, in nanoseconds per batch, and "lo move" the split's minimum against master's. A move is
read against the band of the unchanged code (-band.txt and -128bit-band.txt beside this file).
