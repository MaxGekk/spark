== reading ==
On the native arm, the default since row 478, a byte negation and abs run 3.0x to 3.4x faster in L1 and about
2.1x to 2.4x at 512 KB, at both widths: the byte multiply x86 lacks is gone (NEG is a subtract from zero) and abs
is one ABS instead of a compare, a multiply and a blend. At 2 MB the store becomes memory bound and the gain falls
to 1.15x to 1.26x. On the widened arm abs gains 1.43x to 1.48x everywhere (the compare and blend go; the int
multiply was already cheap), and the negation 1.06x to 1.13x. No cell is slower. One run per cell: the gains are
far outside the control's 7 % spread, but the exact ratios are not repeated.
