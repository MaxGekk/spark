== what the change does ==
At four int lanes a byte column had no widening load: the load read the narrow species of the int
species' own width, and a 32-bit byte species does not exist, so narrowRefusal declined every
byte-driven shape at 128 bits. The load now reads the narrow species of the next width up - an
eight-lane 64-bit byte species - and converts each of its two parts as one int group of the
iteration. The loop computes two groups an iteration, its bound rounds down to a whole iteration,
and the rows past it go through the masked epilogue one group at a time; a narrow store whose
species is wider than a group is always masked, so its last group cannot write past the batch.
Eight, sixteen, 32 and 64 int lanes keep their own species and their bytes.

- Nulls and validity: no batch has a null, so only the dense path is timed.
- The add chain's 2.2 to 2.3 G rows/s plateau in every JVM, and the compare's slow mode in
