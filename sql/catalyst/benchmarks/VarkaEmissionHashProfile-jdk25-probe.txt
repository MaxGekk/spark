## 2. Why no deeper shape reaches the emitter

A chain of year(date_add(greatest(... d ...))) nested to depth 8 fuses; nested to depth 16 the
compiler declines it for the fused budget, which caps the operations in one output. So every
subtree the emitter hashes is bounded by that budget, and a lookup's cost with it.
